Seccion 04 (extra) - Limitar la carga de Ollama con un override de systemd
Integrantes: Angel Arcos - Sebastian Coral - Juan Orjuela - Javier Rosero | 2026-09-24 20:47:00 | ubuntu-ollama
==================================================================

Motivo: con varios modelos cargados a la vez la VM (8 GB) se saturo y dejo de responder.

cloud@ubuntu-ollama:~$ sudo mkdir -p /etc/systemd/system/ollama.service.d
cloud@ubuntu-ollama:~$ cat /etc/systemd/system/ollama.service.d/limites.conf
[Service]
Environment="OLLAMA_MAX_LOADED_MODELS=1"
Environment="OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL=1"

cloud@ubuntu-ollama:~$ sudo systemctl daemon-reload && sudo systemctl restart ollama

cloud@ubuntu-ollama:~$ systemctl cat ollama
# /etc/systemd/system/ollama.service
[Unit]
Description=Ollama Service
After=network-online.target

[Service]
ExecStart=/usr/local/bin/ollama serve
User=ollama
Group=ollama
Restart=always
RestartSec=3
Environment="PATH=/usr/local/sbin:/usr/local/bin:/usr/sbin:/usr/bin:/sbin:/bin:/snap/bin"

[Install]
WantedBy=default.target

# /etc/systemd/system/ollama.service.d/limites.conf
[Service]
Environment="OLLAMA_MAX_LOADED_MODELS=1"
Environment="OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL=1"

cloud@ubuntu-ollama:~$ systemctl show ollama -p Environment
Environment=PATH=/usr/local/sbin:/usr/local/bin:/usr/sbin:/usr/bin:/sbin:/bin:/snap/bin OLLAMA_MAX_LOADED_MODELS=1 OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL=1

cloud@ubuntu-ollama:~$ systemctl is-active ollama
active
